How It Works

A clear delivery journey, designed around privacy and convenience.

STEP 01

Place an order

The customer selects products and preferred and alternative hubs.

STEP 02

Seller prepares

The seller prepares the parcel and marks it ready for dispatch.

STEP 03

Parcel is deposited

An authorized rider deposits the parcel in a suitable assigned locker.

STEP 04

Secure collection

After payment verification, the customer receives pickup authorization for the collection flow.

Privacy is part of the design.

For locker deliveries, seller and rider views are intended to show only the information needed to process a parcel—not the customer’s home address or personal phone number. This prototype demonstrates the idea and requires further security work before real use.
